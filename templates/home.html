Questions

Good to know.

Are my conversations private?

Your saved conversations are tied to your account. Files you upload are processed in memory and sent to the configured AI provider for analysis, so avoid uploading sensitive or confidential information.

Which files can I upload?

TXT, Markdown, CSV, JSON, LOG, PDF, DOCX and XLSX, up to 5 MB, in the Document and Data assistants.

Can I use ConnectAI Hub on my phone?

Yes. It works in any modern browser and can be installed to your home screen from the browser menu.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. From account settings you can export your data, delete your AI history, or delete your account.
